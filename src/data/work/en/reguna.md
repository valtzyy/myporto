## The problem

Restaurants, markets and hotels throw away organic waste that has real value to
someone else — banana peels, vegetable trimmings, spent coffee grounds, used
cooking oil, crushed eggshells. The recyclers, animal feed producers and compost
makers who want that material have no reliable way to find it, and the suppliers
have no reason to sort it if no buyer exists.

Neither side moves first. That is a marketplace problem, not a recycling one.

## What it does

ReGuna is a two-sided marketplace connecting food-waste suppliers with the
businesses that process it. Suppliers list organic waste by category with a
price per kilogram; buyers browse a catalogue organised by material type —
processing residue, kitchen trimmings, fruit and peel, shells and hard casings,
used cooking oil.

The platform handles registration and authentication for both sides, seller
catalogues, and the product listings that sit between them.

## Where it stands

Live at [veternity-app.fly.dev](https://veternity-app.fly.dev), deployed on
Fly.io. The catalogue is populated and the listings carry real category and
pricing structure. Supplier numbers are still small — this is an early-stage
platform, and the honest description of it is a working product looking for
liquidity, not a solved market.
